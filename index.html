Overview

Purpose

Deliver a welcoming first interaction that builds trust and foundation for positive relationship

Benefit to Dealership

  • Smooth guided transition into the showroom
  • Accurate lead data recorded at source enabling better lead management

Benefit to Customer

  • Convenient valet parking and vehicle handling from arrival to departure
  • Warm welcome and minimal wait-time at comfortable lounge
  • Profile verification ensuring personalized assistance matching customer preferences

JSW Motors Process Standards

1. Open the showroom door

Who
Security Guard
How

Smile with eye contact and open the showroom door for all customers coming in and going out.

2. Welcome the customer and introduce yourself

Who
Hostess
How

Spot the entering customer and introduce yourself by name:

“Hello sir/madam, Welcome to JSW Motors! I am [Hostess Name], your Hostess for the day. It is wonderful to have you here. Could you please share your phone number with me? This helps us ensure our team has all the details to make your JSW Motors journey effortless.”

Enabler
TabletHostess app

3. Request the customer to scan the QR code and confirm their vehicle of interest ★

Who
Hostess
How

Walk the customer to the reception desk.

Request them to scan the QR code and fill their name and phone number. Once the QR form is submitted by the customer, the enquiry will be visible in hostess tab.

Using your tablet or phone, on the hostess app, verify the customer’s vehicle of interest.

Enabler
QR code

3.1 Manually fill the enquiry form

Who
Hostess
How

If the customer does not scan the QR code, create the lead manually by entering the customer’s name and phone number in the hostess app.

Enabler
TabletHostess app

3.2 Walk the customer to the lounge and offer seating

Who
Hostess
How

Personally walk the customer to the seating lounge and offer seating.

Proactively request the pantry staff to serve drinking water.

Ensure that the pantry staff serves water in sealed glass bottles instead of plastic bottles to align with the brand’s sustainability DNA.

4. Share the menu card and confirm choice of beverage

Who
Hostess
How

Share the menu card with the customer and confirm their choice of beverage:

"Here's our beverage menu, sir/madam. Please let me know what we can serve you today. We'll have it ready in a few minutes."

Enabler
Menu cardF&B checklist

5. Request the pantry staff to serve beverage as per customer’s preference, ensuring it’s served on time

Who
Hostess
How

Ensure that the pantry staff serves the beverage only in JSW Motors branded high-quality crockery.

Inform the pantry staff about the customer’s choice of beverage

6. Inform the customer that sales consultant will join them shortly

Who
Hostess
How

Inform the customer that a Sales Consultant will join them shortly and, in the meanwhile, they may engage with the brochures and reading material available in the seating lounge.

"Your Sales Consultant will join you shortly. In the meantime, do make yourself comfortable. There are brochures and reading material on the table."

Ensure that the pantry staff serves beverages on time without keeping customers waiting for long.

7. Check the Sales Consultant’s availability on the floor and on the app

Who
Hostess
How

Check Sales Consultant availability on the floor and on the app. Available time slots without any prior commitment of appointment or test drive will be visible on the hostess app.

Enabler
Hostess app

7.1 Handover customer details to the SC and assign them the enquiry on the app

Who
Hostess
How

While the customer is seated in the lounge, walk up to the available SC and inform them about the customer.

Based on their availability, assign them the lead on the app and block their calendar.

Handover the basic details like name, time when they arrived, what refreshments they were offered, and their vehicle of interest.

"[SC Name], you have a customer waiting. Mr/Mrs [Customer Name] walked in at [time] and is seated in the lounge at [seat/table number]. Their vehicle of interest is [model name]. This is an [Appointment/Walk-in] customer. They're accompanied by [number] guests. Please do join them - I've told them you'll be with them shortly and in the meanwhile we served them a cold drink as they especially requested for it due to the heat today."

Enabler
Hostess appSC app

7.2 Communicate SC availability timeline to the customer

Who
Hostess
How

Proactively communicate the SC wait time to the customer.

If all the SCs are unavailable beyond the committed window, the Team Lead followed by the Sales Manager must engage with the customer.

8. Showroom feedback is auto-triggered

Who
System
How

Trigger 1 of Voice to Text WhatsApp feedback:

4 hours after submitting QR code form at showroom visit without test drive

1 hour after TD ends OTP verification

If no response on trigger 1, trigger 2 of star rating feedback link sent on WhatsApp and Email:

16 hours after showroom visit without test drive

13 hours after TD ends OTP verification

If no response on trigger 2, trigger 3 of call centre calling the customer for feedback collection:

40 hours after showroom visit without test drive

37 hours after TD ends OTP verification

The feeback loop closes when the response is received on any one of the above feedback triggers.

Trigger 3 is the last follow up for feedback.

In case, the customer visits multiple times, feedback is triggered only for the first visit.

Enabler
Showroom feedback questions
