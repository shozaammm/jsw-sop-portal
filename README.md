## 📋 Architecture & Elements

- **Executive Branding & Design System:** JSW Motors luxury automotive executive styling, Plus Jakarta Sans & Manrope typography, JetBrains Mono for formula & code notation, and champagne gold accent palette.
- **Foundational Pillars:**
  - Customer Expectations
  - Objective
  - JSW Differentiator
  - Enablers (Tools & Checklists)
- **Interactive Process Flow Canvas:**
  - Real-time journey mapping: Step 1 (Security Guard) → Step 2 (Valet) → Reception Gateway (Decision) → Step 3 (Hostess, JSW Eminence) & Step 4/5 (Sales Consultant).
  - Direct step synchronization: Clicking any step in the process flow opens that specific step row exclusively in the Process Standards section.
- **The JSW Process Standards:**
  - Dual Mode: Interactive Step Inspector Cards & Complete Executive Standards Matrix Table (What, Who, How, Enablers).
  - JSW Eminence distinction for Hostess showroom reception.
- **Success Metrics (KPIs):**
  - KPI 1: Formula `(a+b+c)/y` with Security Guard ownership & live simulation sandbox.
  - KPI 2: Formula `(x/y)*100` with Hostess ownership & live simulation sandbox.
- **Certified Zero-Hallucination:** Every single role, paragraph, step, enabler, formula, and example is derived strictly from `JSW SOP Elements.docx`.
